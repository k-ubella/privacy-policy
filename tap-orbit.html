Tap Orbit Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 6, 2026 · Last updated: September 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Tap Orbit (package name: com.taporbit.arcade) handles information when you play the game, use the optional global leaderboard, or view rewarded advertising.

1. Information we collect

Global leaderboard (optional). If you choose to join the global leaderboard, we collect and store an app-generated random player ID, your nickname, score, 30-second play duration, and score submission time. The nickname and score are shown publicly to other Tap Orbit players in the leaderboard.

Local game data. Your device stores your local best score, leaderboard preference, and leaderboard ID. This data is not transferred until you choose to join the leaderboard.

No account or direct contact information. Tap Orbit does not require an account and does not directly collect your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, or payment information.

2. Why we use information

  • To operate the global weekly leaderboard, prevent obvious invalid scores, and allow you to see your rank.
  • To provide game progress stored on your device.
  • To provide rewarded ads, measure ad delivery, prevent fraud, and keep the app reliable.

3. Advertising and third parties

Tap Orbit uses Google AdMob for rewarded advertising. Google and AdMob may process advertising identifiers, IP address, device and network details, ad interaction events, app performance, and diagnostic information for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, and, where permitted, ad personalization. Choices about personalized ads and consent can be managed through device settings or Google's consent tools. See the Google Privacy Policy and AdMob privacy information.

Leaderboard records are processed by our cloud hosting and database service providers solely to operate the leaderboard. We do not sell personal data.

4. Sharing and public visibility

Only the nickname and score that you submit to the global leaderboard are visible to other players. We do not display your email address, advertising ID, IP address, or app-generated player ID. We do not sell or rent leaderboard information.

5. Retention and deletion

Leaderboard data is retained while the leaderboard service is operated, unless you delete it. You can delete your nickname and all submitted leaderboard scores at any time in the app: open RANK, then choose Delete leaderboard data. This permanently removes the leaderboard profile and associated scores from our database. Local game data can be removed by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

6. Security and children

Leaderboard data is transmitted over HTTPS. No system can guarantee absolute security. Tap Orbit is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

7. Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the app or legal requirements change. The updated policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date. For privacy questions or deletion assistance, contact fakehuman755@gmail.com.

탭 오빗 개인정보처리방침

시행일: 2026년 9월 6일 · 최종 수정일: 2026년 9월 6일

탭 오빗(패키지 이름: com.taporbit.arcade)은 게임, 선택형 글로벌 랭킹 및 보상형 광고를 제공합니다.

1. 처리하는 정보

글로벌 랭킹 참여를 선택한 경우 앱이 생성한 임의의 플레이어 ID, 닉네임, 점수, 30초 플레이 시간 및 제출 시간을 저장합니다. 이 중 닉네임과 점수만 다른 플레이어에게 공개됩니다. 기기 내 최고 점수와 랭킹 참여 설정은 기기에 저장됩니다.

회원가입은 필요하지 않으며, 개발자는 이름, 이메일 주소, 전화번호, 정밀 위치, 연락처 또는 결제 정보를 직접 수집하지 않습니다.

2. 이용 목적 및 제3자

랭킹 제공, 비정상 점수의 기본 검증, 게임 진행 저장 및 보상형 광고 제공을 위해 정보를 이용합니다. 보상형 광고는 Google AdMob이 제공하며 광고 식별자, IP 주소, 기기·네트워크 정보, 광고 상호작용 및 진단 정보를 처리할 수 있습니다. 랭킹 데이터는 랭킹 운영을 위한 클라우드 호스팅·데이터베이스 제공업체에서만 처리됩니다. 개인정보를 판매하지 않습니다.

3. 보관 및 삭제

랭킹 데이터는 서비스 운영 중 보관되며, 앱의 RANK → Delete leaderboard data에서 언제든지 닉네임과 제출 점수를 영구 삭제할 수 있습니다. 기기 내 데이터는 앱 저장공간을 삭제하거나 앱을 삭제하면 제거할 수 있습니다.
